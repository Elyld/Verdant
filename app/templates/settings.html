Settings

Your garden location, frost dates, and the morning digest.

📍 Garden location

Don't know your zone? Look it up at planthardiness.ars.usda.gov. Zone dates are approximate averages — good enough for countdowns and sowing calendars. An exact date always wins over the zone.

Coordinates power the Planner's weather strip and stamp new observations with the current conditions (free Open-Meteo data, no key needed). They take precedence over the GARDEN_LAT / GARDEN_LON env vars. Use my location first tries your browser's location (needs a secure context and your permission); if that's unavailable it does a one-time city-level lookup via ip-api.com. It only ever runs when you click it — nothing is stored beyond the coordinates themselves.

Frost preview will appear here once you save.

✨ Preferences

The week-start day rearranges the calendar columns; the weight unit prefills the harvest form; the confirm toggle guards against accidental taps of “Water all”.

📱 Mobile tab bar

Pick up to 4 sections for the bottom tab bar on your phone, in the order you want them — tap a chip to add it, tap again to remove. Everything else stays one tap away under More.

🤖 Local AI

Let a small model running on your own machine turn a sentence like “watered the tomatoes and harvested 3 peppers” into log entries. Nothing leaves your server — the app just talks to the model over your local network. Off by default.

Verdant runs in Docker, so localhost here means the Verdant container itself — not your PC. To reach Ollama on the same machine, use http://host.docker.internal:11434, and set OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 on Ollama so it accepts the connection. No /v1 needed — the app adds the /api/… paths itself.

Works with any Ollama-compatible server (/api/tags and /api/chat). A 3–4B model is plenty — it only parses sentences into draft entries, which you confirm before anything is saved.

🌅 Morning digest

In Discord: Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook, pick a channel, then “Copy Webhook URL”. Changes apply immediately — no restart needed.
